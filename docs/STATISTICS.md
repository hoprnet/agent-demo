# Statistics helpers — a comprehensive overview

In today's fast-paced world of data, it's important to note that statistics serve as a crucial cornerstone for understanding. This module leverages a robust, seamless suite of helpers that empower developers to unlock insights — not just numbers, but meaning.

The mean is utilized in order to basically compute the average, which is essentially the typical value, and it's really quite useful, and it raises an error if the list is empty, which is important.

Variance and standard deviation delve into the spread. Sample variance divides by n-1 while population variance divides by n, and the difference matters because of degrees of freedom which is a concept that relates to the number of independent pieces of information that go into the estimate, which is why we lose one when we estimate the mean from the same data.
